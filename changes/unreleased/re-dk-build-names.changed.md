- **The two `sysml` builds are named after the halves of the REDK.** The default build with every feature group is the DK (Development Kit) build; the build without the optional and developer-only groups, formerly the "production" build, is the RE (Runtime Environment) build: `-tags sysml_re` replaces `-tags sysml_prod`, `make build-re` and `make build-wasm-re` replace `make build-prod` and `make build-wasm-prod`, and they write `bin/sysml-re` and `sysml-re.wasm`. What each build links is unchanged, as are the per-group `sysml_no<group>` tags.
