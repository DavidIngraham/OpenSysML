- **Boundary parameter symbols of a positioned node no longer coincide.** In the interconnection rendering of a view with `DiagramLayout::Layout` positions, a use case's `actor` and `subject` parameters are boundary symbols placed where their connections' routes meet the node, so two routes ending at one point drew the symbols, and their labels, over each other. Symbols the routes of one side place over one another are now spread along that side, touching the border, in the order the routes come from, and each route ends on its own symbol; a symbol overlapping none of its side stays where its route ends. This is the shared layout of the `dot` form and the Cameo style; the other forms carry no positions.
- **The SysML v1 migrator marks the connection names it makes up.** A connection written for an anonymous v1 association, `connection 'Driver to Park Car' connect …`, is named after its ends, and a connection usage typed by a connection def is named after the def, but neither name was recorded as `MigrationMetadata::SynthesizedName`, so the rendering labelled the line with the invented name where the source tool drew none. The migration now marks them, and the renderer's existing rule leaves the edge unlabelled, or labelled by its type; a named v1 association keeps its label.
